NOTESAssumptions, mechanics + scoring spec
READER
  • One ayah at a time, held still and centred. Nothing moves on its own — the reader advances it.
  • No autoplay, no speed control. → / ← keys or the two hairline arrows under the text; that is the whole interaction.
  • The arrows are deliberately near‑invisible: the ayah is the only loud thing on the page.
  • A verse is credited when the reader moves forward off it. Going back does not re‑credit.
  • Translation is a fixed sans, size‑adjustable only. Arabic gets font + size.
REVERSE TIMER
  • Three fixed session lengths, each framed as an everyday act so the cost feels trivially small: 0:30 “a glass of water”, 1:10 “boiling the kettle”, 2:20 “frying an egg”.
  • The clock counts down while the bar beside it fills up. The number shrinks, the bar grows — the same session read two ways.
  • Hitting 0:00 closes the session and banks the points. Nothing is lost if you stop early; the bar just stops where it stopped.
  • The countdown runs for as long as the reader is open. Closing the tab keeps whatever the session already earned.
SCORING — DAILY BEATS BINGE
Per‑verse value scales with the rung you committed to, then a streak multiplier lands on top.
